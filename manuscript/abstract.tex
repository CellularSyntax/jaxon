Peripheral nerve stimulation is a rapidly expanding therapeutic
modality, but optimising electrode geometry and stimulus waveforms
for fascicle-specific selectivity remains computationally expensive:
state-of-the-art pipelines wrap the NEURON simulator from Python and
require thousands of forward simulations per design candidate.
We present \emph{jaxon}, a fully differentiable reimplementation of
the canonical peripheral nerve fiber models---McIntyre--Richardson--Grill
(MRG) myelinated axons, and the unmyelinated Sundt, Sweeney and Rattay
C-fibers---in JAX.  Built on top of the \texttt{jaxley} differentiable
Hodgkin--Huxley framework, jaxon adds a batched coupled-cable
backward-Euler integrator that simulates hundreds of fibers in
parallel on a single GPU and exposes end-to-end gradients with respect
to per-contact stimulus amplitudes and arbitrary waveforms.
We validate jaxon against \texttt{pyfibers}-wrapped NEURON on
strength--duration thresholds across $D=0.3$--$16\,\mu\mathrm{m}$, eight
stimulus waveforms and pulse widths from $0.02$ to $1\,\mathrm{ms}$:
\textbf{$99.6\,\%$ of 943 test configurations agree to within $1\%$
relative threshold error}, with peak errors of 0.39\,\% (MRG, 432
configs), 0.46\,\% (Sundt, 240), 0.06\,\% (Sweeney, 31), and 2.24\,\%
(Rattay, 240; four biphasic-cathodic edge cases at the shortest pulse
width exceed $1\%$).  Conduction velocity matches NEURON to within
$0.24\,\%$ across all models.  Benchmarking throughput
against pyfibers on a single NVIDIA A100, jaxon achieves a
\textbf{geometric-mean speedup of $\sim 820\times$ at $N=100\,000$
across all five implemented axon models}, ranging from $527\times$
on MRG-interpolated to $1\,377\times$ on Sweeney
($5\,972$\,s\,$\to$\,4.3\,s), with $1\,006\times$ on Sundt and
$929\times$ on Rattay C-fibers; jaxon crosses parity with pyfibers
at $N\approx 10$ and dominates thereafter.  As a
demonstration of the differentiability advantage, we use jaxon to
optimise spatially selective fascicular stimulation across 125
randomised Hussain-style multi-fascicle nerve realisations spanning
all four fibre models (25 seeds each, every seed with an independently
randomised target / off-target divider angle): rectangular-pulse
amplitude optimisation achieves \textbf{median $\mathrm{SI}=1.000$
on every model}, with cross-model mean SI of $0.955$ and
$70\,\%$ of seeds reaching the theoretical maximum
$\mathrm{SI}=1.0$ (all target fibres firing, all off-target fibres
silent).
jaxon is open-source and designed to slot into existing peripheral nerve
stimulation pipelines as a drop-in replacement for the NEURON forward
solver, with the additional capability of gradient-based optimisation.
